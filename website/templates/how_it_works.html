SIMPLE PROCESS

Give Knowledge. Get Knowledge.

GyanBarter is a cashless community. You earn credits by teaching what you love, and spend them to learn what you need. It's that simple.

Your Journey in 4 Steps

1. Create Profile

Sign up and list your skills. Tell us what you can teach (e.g. Python) and what you want to learn (e.g. Guitar).

2. Post a Session

Create a listing for your expertise. Set your availability and describe what you will teach.

3. Teach & Earn

Conduct the session via video call. Once done, you earn GyanCredits in your wallet.

4. Spend & Learn

Use your credits to book experts and learn new skills for free. The cycle continues!

The GyanCredit System

Our economy is based on time, not money. We ensure fair exchange through a simple credit system.

1 Hour Teaching = +1 Credit Earned
1 Hour Learning = -1 Credit Spent
Sign Up Bonus = 50 Free Credits

1 Credit = 1 Hour

No hidden fees. 100% Transparent.
